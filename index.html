# How we work

  1. 01

    Play first

    We pick a shiny new technology and poke it until it makes sense.

  2. 02

    AI-assisted, human-led

    AI writes, reviews and suggests. People decide, test and sign off. We test every change and ask for independent reviews.

  3. 03

    Safe by design

    No secrets in public repos, no trackers on this site, and we measure before we trust.

  4. 04

    Explain it simply

    If we can't explain it to a friend over coffee, we don't understand it yet.
